Common questions

Is this a diagnosis?
No. FocusCheck is a screening tool, not a diagnostic instrument. It can tell you whether your symptoms are consistent with ADHD, but only a qualified healthcare professional can diagnose ADHD after a proper evaluation.
How accurate is the ASRS?
The Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale was developed by the World Health Organization and is one of the most widely used screening instruments in adult ADHD research. It is a strong first indicator, but it is designed to flag who should seek a professional evaluation — not to replace one.
Do I need to give my email?
No. You can complete the assessment and see your results without providing any personal information. An email is only needed if you purchase the detailed report.
What happens to my answers?
They stay in your browser. We do not sell data, and we do not share your individual responses with third parties.
What if my result suggests ADHD?
It means a professional evaluation is worth pursuing. The full report explains your specific profile and how to bring it up with a doctor or psychologist — many people find that a written summary makes the conversation much easier.
